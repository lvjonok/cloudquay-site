CloudQuay is an independent macOS app that connects NAS shares and Google Drive to Finder. Browse folders, open files on demand, and copy chosen files onto your Mac.

CloudQuay is currently a personal-use development project. It is not a publicly verified Google application or a notarized public release.

Files stay on your storage

Connecting a drive does not sync your whole account. CloudQuay lists file metadata and caches content requested by Finder or your applications. Open files and pending uploads can temporarily exceed the configured cache target.

Your Google connection

Sign in using Google's browser authorization page. New connections request read-only access by default. If you select read-and-write access before authorizing, CloudQuay requests permission to modify your Drive files. You can revoke access in your Google Account.

Runs on your Mac

The SwiftUI app uses rclone for Google Drive and FTP connections and native macOS mounting for SMB shares. Google tokens, connection settings, and cached content are stored locally. CloudQuay does not operate a remote file-processing service.
